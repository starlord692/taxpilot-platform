## Approved Decision

For Business Momentum v1.0, the six approved core dimensions are treated equally:

1. Financial Performance
2. Cash & Receivables
3. Expenses
4. Operations
5. Customers
6. Suppliers

At least three valid core dimensions are required to determine a whole-business direction.

- A strict majority of valid core dimensions classified as `IMPROVING` produces `IMPROVING`.
- A strict majority of valid core dimensions classified as `DETERIORATING` produces `DETERIORATING`.
- Otherwise, where sufficient valid evidence exists, the result is `BROADLY_UNCHANGED`.
- Fewer than three valid core dimensions produces `INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION`.

No single dimension may override the result. Hidden weighting and scoring are prohibited.

The resulting assessment preserves supporting evidence and applicable policy context. AI has no authority to determine, override, weight, score, or otherwise alter the deterministic direction result.

## Scope

This restored record preserves Founder-approved governance only. It does not itself authorize implementation, APIs, persistence, database design, or any other implementation-specific decision.
